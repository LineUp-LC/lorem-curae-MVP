## Founder & Credibility

### Origin Story

- **Why this business exists**: Finding the right skincare shouldn't require hours of research, endless trial and error, or deciphering confusing ingredient lists. Lorem Curae was built to change that—a space where personalized guidance meets transparency, where you can discover products that actually work for your unique skin, from retailers you can trust.
- **Personal connection**: Founder was tired of spending countless hours researching products that didn't work for his skin. He wanted to create a space where everyone could find their perfect skincare match without the overwhelm.
- **Catalyst**: The frustration of the research-to-purchase cycle—spending hours researching only to end up with products that didn't work, with no way to track what was actually helping or hurting.

### Founder Profile

- **Name**: Ethan Jones
- **Age**: 18 years old
- **Background**: Young African American male, currently in high school, solopreneur
- **Role**: Founder & CEO — sole developer, designer, product strategist, and operator
- **Notable**: Built the entire MVP solo while in high school. Self-taught full-stack developer with a production-grade tech stack.
- **Narrative angle**: Gen Z founder who experienced the skincare confusion firsthand and built the solution himself—a compelling underdog/builder story for press, content, and investor narratives.

### Credibility & Proof Points

- Pre-revenue (pre-launch)
- Full MVP built solo — 78 lazy-loaded routes, 15 Supabase Edge Functions, 36 AI modules, Stripe Connect payment infrastructure, comprehensive design system
- Demonstrates exceptional execution for a solo founder of any age

### Unique Edge

- **Unique angle**: No platform currently unifies personalized education with routine tracking, ingredient transparency, and a creator-powered marketplace. Everyone else solves one piece; Lorem Curae solves the whole journey.
- **Unique method**: Adaptive intelligence that evolves with the user—context-aware recommendations, routine guidance, and progress feedback powered by AI (Curae AI), combined with a trust-first UX and creator enablement marketplace.

## Brand Voice

- **Brand personality**: Intelligent, trustworthy, empowering, approachable, inclusive
- **Tone of voice**: Clear, science-informed, empathetic, confident but never condescending. Speaks like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporation.
- **Tone you NEVER use**: Corporate jargon, condescending, hype-driven, fear-mongering, overly clinical

### Voice Examples

- **Tweet**: "Your skin is unique. Your routine should be too. Lorem Curae learns what works for YOU—not what's trending."
- **Customer complaint**: "We hear you, and we're on it. Your experience matters to us—let's make this right together."
- **New feature**: "You asked, we built. Say hello to [feature]—designed to make your skincare journey even smoother."
- **Cold outreach DM**: "Hey [name]—I noticed your passion for clean skincare. We're building something I think you'd love. Mind if I share?"

### Brand Values

1. **Integrity** — Transparent product information, trustworthy seller verification
2. **Empowerment** — Knowledge and tools that put users in control of their journey
3. **Inclusivity** — Solutions for all skin types, tones, and budgets
4. **Sustainability** — Prioritizing ethical brands, mindful consumption, and informed choices
5. **Community** — Enabling peer support and creator-led discovery

### Key Messaging Pillars

1. Personalized intelligence that evolves with you
2. Trust and transparency in every product and recommendation
3. Holistic wellness—skin, nutrition, and lifestyle connected
4. Creator empowerment and community-driven discovery
