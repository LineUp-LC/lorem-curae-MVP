# Business Model, Metrics & Operations

---

## Revenue Model

Hybrid: Freemium subscription + Affiliate commissions + Marketplace fees + E-commerce + Data licensing

### Revenue Streams

**1. Affiliate Model** — 5-10% commission from trusted skincare brands. Optional sponsored visibility. Cashback/credits shared with users.

**2. Marketplace Model** — Flat fee ($1-$3) or 10-20% per order from indie creators/estheticians. Volume incentives for high-performers.

**3. Freemium to Premium (Subscription)** — Free tier: core discovery, ingredient checks, routine tracking, basic insights. Premium: unlimited AR, advanced diagnostics, early access, exclusive discounts, gamified rewards.

**4. E-Commerce** — Direct product sales, curated collections, exclusive bundles, seasonal drops, creator collaborations.

**5. Data Licensing (Future)** — Aggregated, anonymized insights to NGOs, research institutions, ethical brands.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes | Target |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| Standard (Free) | $0 | Limited Curae AI, 1 regimen, limited image submissions, limited Curae Coins | Casual users, new signups |
| Skin Enthusiast | $10-15/mo | Unlimited AI, unlimited regimens, unlimited images, full Curae Coins, faster shipping, AR access | Engaged enthusiasts |
| Enterprise/Creator | TBD | Creator tools, analytics, seller dashboard, monetization features | Indie brands, estheticians |

- **Key conversion metric**: User creates first regimen and receives personalized AI recommendation
- **ACV**: ~$120-180/year (at $10-15/mo)
- **Pricing principles**: Keep core free to maximize adoption. Premium deepens engagement. Reward consistent tracking. Transparent, ethical monetization.

## Resources & Constraints

- **Budget**: Minimal (bootstrapped, high school student)
