# Marketing, Growth & Journey

---

## Customer Journey

| Stage | What Happens | Channel | Success Metric |
|-------|-------------|---------|---------------|
| 1. Awareness | User discovers Lorem Curae | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, SEO, Reddit, Product Hunt | Impressions, reach, views |
| 2. Education | User learns what it does | Landing page, blog, social, email welcome | Site visits, time on page, signups |
| 3. Trust | User builds confidence | Social proof, community, ingredient transparency | Signup rate, free tier activation |
| 4. Conversion | User commits | Free tier onboarding, upgrade prompts, feature gating | Free-to-paid conversion rate |
| 5. Delivery | First customer experience | In-app onboarding, AI interaction, first regimen | Activation rate (first regimen) |
| 6. Retention | User returns and builds habits | Push notifications, progress popups, reminders | DAU/MAU, 30-day retention |
| 7. Referral | User brings others | Referral rewards, social sharing, community | Referral rate, viral coefficient |

### Key Conversion Points

- **Signup trigger**: Seeing a personalized skin analysis or ingredient breakdown for THEIR concern
- **Activation event**: Creates first regimen and receives first personalized AI recommendation
- **Aha moment**: First progress insight or genuinely tailored recommendation (not generic)
- **Upgrade trigger**: Hitting free tier limits while actively engaged

## Growth Channels

### Organic Content / SEO
- **Content pillars**: Ingredient education, routine guides, skincare myth-busting, product comparisons, wellness + skin connection
- **Content types**: Blog posts, guides, case studies, comparisons, tutorials, short-form video

### Social Media (Planned)

| Platform | Content Type |
|----------|-------------|
| TikTok | Short-form educational, ingredient breakdowns, skin transformations |
| Instagram Reels | Visual routines, before/after, product spotlights |
| YouTube Shorts | Quick tips, myth-busting, Curae AI demos |
| Reddit | Community engagement, skincare advice, AMA-style posts |
| X (Twitter) | Industry commentary, product updates, engagement |

### Launch Channels
TikTok (organic), Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, SEO, Reddit, X, Product Hunt, influencers (organic + paid), paid ads (broad targeting)

### Partnerships
- **Target partners**: Dermatologists & estheticians, indie beauty brands, affiliate networks (Skimlinks, Rakuten, Impact), AR/AI tech providers, wellness brands, NGOs & research institutions
- **Value proposition**: Access to targeted, purchase-ready, engaged skincare audience with trust infrastructure

### Growth Levers
Viral AR experiences, creator-led launches, referral/rewards programs, SEO through education, personalized routine retention, marketplace expansion, wellness integrations, international expansion

## Habit Loop Design

### MVP Habit Loops

**Trigger → Action → Reward → Investment**

- **Trigger**: Morning/evening routine reminder
- **Action**: Log routine, check ingredients, get AI recommendation
- **Reward**: Personalized insight, progress update, Curae Coins
- **Investment**: Routine history, skin data, regimen notes (creates switching costs)

### Growth Phase Additions

- Community validation loops (share progress → feedback → motivation)
- Creator content loops (publish → engagement → revenue)
- AR exploration loops (try products virtually → discover → purchase)
